# Credits

All materials in this folder are from [ambientCG](https://ambientcg.com) and are released under
[CC0 1.0](https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/) (public domain). Attribution is not required;
these links are here so each set can be traced and re-downloaded at higher resolution.

The weapon sets lead the shelf and were downloaded at **2K-JPG** for close-up hard-surface work;
the environment support sets are **1K-JPG** to keep the built-in library quick to load. You can
re-fetch any set at 4K by changing its `format` in `tools/materials.json`.
